# Nodra brand direction

Status: implemented frontend direction

A precise infrastructure field journal. Quiet confidence, generous space, visible rules and useful diagrams.

- Canvas: warm paper #f5f4ef; surface #fffefa; ink #20221f; muted #666960; line #d8dad1.
- Accent: burnt orange #b94027. Semantic success: forest #356047. No gradients or shadows.
- Typography: locally served Manrope for headings and interface; IBM Plex Mono for technical labels and identifiers. Large headings use tight spacing; numbers are tabular.
- Geometry: mostly square panels, 4px controls, pill status labels. Structure comes from alignment and thin rules.
- Motion: brief sectional reveals, diagram assembly, chart stroke reveal and tactile button transitions. Honor reduced motion. No perpetual decorative motion or scroll hijacking.
- Voice: concrete and measured. Never imply synthetic data is live, independently verified or a guaranteed return.
